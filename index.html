How it works

Up and running in an afternoon

  1. 1Set up

    Build your intake form

    Answer a few questions about your practice and Circle generates a consult request form in your colors, with your language. No site rebuild, no developer.

  2. 2Share

    Add the link to your site

    Drop your Circle link on your website, directory profile, or email signature. Clients see one calm page instead of a phone number and a hope.

  3. 3Respond

    Review requests in one place

    Every consult request lands in a tidy inbox with the details you asked for — fit, insurance, availability — so you can reply or waitlist in a minute.

What you get

Small tools that quietly hold the front door

No directory listings, no lead marketplace. Circle only handles the people already trying to reach you.

  • Custom consult questions

    Ask what actually determines fit — modality, insurance, session times — and stop trading six emails before the first call.

  • One organized inbox

    New, replied, waitlisted. Every request keeps its status so nothing quietly falls through.

  • Waitlist that stays warm

    Hold clients you cannot see yet, then reach back out the moment a slot opens — without digging through your inbox.

  • Branding that matches you

    Your logo, palette, and voice on the intake page. It reads like part of your practice, not a third-party form.

  • Private by default

    Encrypted in transit and at rest, minimal data collection, and no client information sold or shared. Ever.

  • Availability controls

    Pause intake when you are full, cap requests per week, and turn the form back on when you have room again.
